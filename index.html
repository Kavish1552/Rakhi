Chapter 1

Navindada's Swing

Ek jhoola. Do dawedaar. Roz jhagda.

Remember the little swing in the hallway at Navindada's house? The one we fought over like it was the last swing on Earth? Whoever got there first ruled the kingdom — and somehow you always got there first.

For the record, Prachi: I still maintain it was my turn.

Chapter 2

Terrace Nights

Chhat, chaandni, aur saare cousins.

Every time all the cousins landed at Nani and Navindada's house, the terrace became our world. Mattresses in a row, everyone talking over everyone, laughing at nothing until someone from downstairs yelled at us to sleep.

No AC, no phones, no plans — and still some of the best nights we've ever had. If I could bottle one feeling from childhood, it would be that terrace under the stars, with all of us together.

Chapter 3

From Chaos to Grace

The evolution of Prachi — a scientific study 🔬

Let it be documented: the girl who was the most chaotic, adventurous troublemaker at Navindada's house — the one always up to something — is now the calmest, warmest, most amazing mother.

Honestly, nobody saw that plot twist coming. But watching you become a mom has been the proudest, sweetest thing, Prachi. Your little one has no idea how lucky they are — they got the fun one AND the wise one in a single parent.

Yeh dhaaga kabhi khula hi nahi.

The thread you tied that day never really came off.

A Letter From Germany

💌tap to open

Dear Prachi,

This is the part where I'm supposed to be at your door with sweets, and you're supposed to tie the rakhi and then very sweetly extort a gift out of me. Instead, there's an ocean between Germany and the USA — so this website will have to carry the thread this year.

Whenever I think of us, I end up back at Navindada's house. The swing in the hallway that we treated like disputed territory. The terrace where all the cousins slept in one long row, laughing until we got scolded. Those days built something no distance can touch.

And look at you now. The most chaotic, adventurous kid of the whole gang became the most amazing mother — patient, warm, and still fun. I don't say it enough, but I'm so proud of the person you've become.

Rakhi is a promise, and mine doesn't need me to be in the same country: I've got your back, always. Whatever the hour, whatever the time zone — call, and I'm there.

Bhai-behen ka pyaar doesn't check passports.

Happy Rakshabandhan, Prachi.
— Kavish 🪢
